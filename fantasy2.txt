<p>Before Arin could step closer, the ground trembled beneath him. From the roots of the oldest tree in the clearing, a massive figure emerged: a guardian of bark and vine, with eyes glowing like embers. Its voice rolled like thunder: &ldquo;Why do you disturb my slumber, child of men?&rdquo;</p>
<p>Arin&rsquo;s knees buckled. &ldquo;I&hellip; I seek a wish. To save my village from the blight,&rdquo; he said, trying to sound braver than he felt. The guardian studied him, its expression unreadable. &ldquo;Many come for selfish reasons. Few for the pure of heart. Tell me, boy, why should I trust you?&rdquo;</p>
<p>The silver-eyed fox leapt onto the guardian&rsquo;s arm, chittering urgently. Slowly, the guardian knelt, lowering its towering head. &ldquo;Ah&hellip; you bring companions. Perhaps there is hope.&rdquo;</p>
<p>Arin explained the blight&mdash;a creeping rot that had begun to kill crops and poison streams. As he spoke, the guardian&rsquo;s vines shifted, creating an intricate tapestry that depicted the village, the forest, and an ominous shadow growing over both. &ldquo;The Heart can grant wishes, yes, but all power comes with a price,&rdquo; the guardian warned.</p>
<p>Suddenly, the crystal in the clearing shattered into shards of pure light. Each shard swirled around Arin and the fox, forming a cage of luminous energy. &ldquo;To claim the wish, you must face your deepest fear,&rdquo; the guardian intoned.</p>
<p>Before Arin could react, he felt himself pulled into a vision&mdash;a forest set ablaze, his village in flames, and shadows consuming everything he loved. His charm pulsed with warmth, and he clenched it tightly, realizing courage alone would not be enough. He would have to outwit the fear, or lose everything.</p>
